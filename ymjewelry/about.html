About Us

A Family of Jewelers

We are a family-owned jewelry business that has been serving the Roanoke Valley for over a decade.

Our jeweler brings more than 30 years of experience to the bench, having worked with clients in Cuba and Italy.

Our core offering is custom jewelry, created to your specifications and your budget. Whether you're dreaming up a hand-made engagement ring, restoring an heirloom that's been in the family for generations, or turning old gold into something new, everything is designed and crafted with the same care we'd give our own family's jewelry.

We believe every piece of jewelry should be something personal — that's why custom design is at the heart of what we do.

  • Hand-made engagement rings — designed and fabricated in-house
  • Custom pieces — set with diamonds, sapphires, rubies, and natural stones
  • Antique jewelry restoration — bringing heirlooms back to life
  • Jewelry repair — sizing, prongs, chains, and stone work
  • Gold recycling — give old gold a new purpose
  • Certified appraisals — for insurance and estates
