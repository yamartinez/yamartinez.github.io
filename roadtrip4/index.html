Pacific Coast road trip

Four days, one car, and roughly 1,500 miles of the most beautiful coastline in the Pacific Northwest. Thor's Well swallowing the tide. Redwoods older than written language. Fog peeling off cliffs at sunrise.

Every meal is mapped — vegan, gluten-free, actually good. Every stop is in order, the whole way down. Just bring snacks.

Now approaching Seattle, WA
5:00 PM · Leaving Seattle

The road south.

Beat rush hour if we can, point the car south, and watch the city turn into trees. Sleep somewhere near Yachats so we wake up two minutes from the ocean.

Stop 1 · Yachats, OR

Thor's Well ★

📍 Open in Maps ~30 min

A sinkhole in the basalt that looks like the ocean draining out of the world. Best near high tide — we'll time it. Stay back from the edge, it's genuinely dangerous and genuinely worth it.

Stop 2 · Yachats, OR

Cape Perpetua ★

📍 Open in Maps

The highest point on the Oregon Coast Highway — 800 feet up, with a view that makes the whole drive feel small. Quick stretch of the legs, big payoff.

Stop 3 · Florence, OR

Heceta Head Lighthouse

📍 Open in Maps

Possibly the most photographed lighthouse on the West Coast, and it's not even close to overrated. Short walk up from the parking area.

Stop 4 · Florence, OR

Oregon Dunes Overlook

📍 Open in Maps

Towering sand dunes that roll straight into the Pacific — the most "wait, we're still in Oregon?" stop of the day. Lunch after at The Tin Thistle in North Bend 🌱GF — real vegan and gluten-free options, not an afterthought menu.

Stop 5 · Bandon, OR

Face Rock

📍 Open in Maps

A sea stack off Bandon that local legend says is a Native princess turned to stone by a sea monster. Great light in the late afternoon.

Stop 6 · Brookings, OR

Secret Beach ★

📍 Open in Maps

A short, slightly steep trail through the forest dumps you onto a cove most people driving 101 have no idea exists. Sea stacks, waterfalls, almost nobody around.

Stop 7 · Brookings, OR

Natural Bridges ★

📍 Open in Maps

Sea arches carved straight through the headland, water glowing turquoise underneath. One of the prettiest two-minute walks on the whole trip.

Stop 8 · Brookings, OR

Arch Rock

📍 Open in Maps

Last big viewpoint before we cross into California. Good place to watch the sun start dropping toward the water.

Crescent City, CA

Dinner, then the redwoods.

Good Harvest Cafe 🌱GF for dinner, then we camp at Jedediah Smith — or a quiet National Forest pullout along US-199 — backed right up against the trees, ready for sunrise.

Sunrise · Crescent City, CA

Enderts Beach ★

📍 Open in Maps

Coffee, a cliff, and the sun coming up over the Pacific. If you can drag yourself out of the car for one sunrise this trip, make it this one.

Crescent City, CA

Stout Grove ★

📍 Open in Maps

One of the most beautiful redwood groves anywhere in California, full stop. Flat, easy loop — just look up the whole time.

Crescent City, CA

Howland Hill Road ★

📍 Open in Maps

An unpaved road that tunnels straight through old-growth redwoods. Slow, narrow, and completely worth the drive — windows down, no music needed.

Crescent City, CA

Boy Scout Tree Trail ★

📍 Open in Maps 4.8 mi round trip

The real hike of the trip. Quiet, mossy, almost nobody else on it. Pack the picnic lunch in — we'll eat it on the trail. (Pack food before we head in — nowhere to grab anything once we're deep in the redwoods.)

Afternoon: Pick Your Adventure

Both options are incredible. We can only realistically do one — vote below.

Fern Canyon ★

Sheer 50-foot walls completely covered in ferns — yes, this is the Jurassic Park canyon. 📍 Open in Maps

⚠️ Needs a reservation + day-use fee for Gold Bluffs Beach access. Reserve →

Gold Bluffs Beach

Wild beach backed by golden cliffs, usually scattered with Roosevelt elk doing absolutely nothing and looking incredible while doing it. 📍 Open in Maps

Tall Trees Grove

Home to some of the tallest trees on the planet. Quiet, remote, far fewer people make it out here. 📍 Open in Maps

⚠️ Free reservation required year-round, gate code emailed beforehand. Reserve →

Lady Bird Johnson Grove

An easy 1.5-mile loop through a grove named for the woman who helped get this whole park protected. Calm, mossy, easy walking after a big morning. 📍 Open in Maps

Golden hour

Sunset, same coast.

Back to Enderts Beach or the Crescent Beach Overlook — whichever we're closer to — to bookend the day the way we started it. Then one more push inland toward Grants Pass so the drive home is shorter.

Optional stop · Ashland, OR

Lithia Park

📍 Open in Maps

Breakfast first at Pangea 🌱GF — fully vegan, gluten-free options that don't feel like an apology. Then a short, easy walk through one of the prettiest little city parks on the coast before seven hours in the car.
