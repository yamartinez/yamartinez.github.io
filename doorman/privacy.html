Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Program: Doorman
Operator: Doorman
Purpose: Private, transactional SMS notifications about the use of a building-entry PIN.

This Privacy Policy explains how Doorman collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its private SMS notification service. The service is not a public marketing list and is currently used only to send transactional entry notifications to the operator's own mobile number or another number that has been expressly configured and authorized by the operator.

Information collected

Doorman may process the following information:

  • A mobile phone number designated to receive SMS notifications.
  • A label or name associated with an authorized entry PIN.
  • The date and time an entry PIN is used.
  • Technical delivery information, such as message status, carrier responses, and error logs.
  • Limited call metadata needed to operate the entry-notification workflow.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Send requested building-entry notifications.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and secure the notification workflow.
  • Maintain records of consent, delivery, opt-out requests, and technical errors.
  • Comply with applicable law, carrier rules, and messaging-platform requirements.

SMS disclosures

Message frequency varies based on entry activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. You may also contact doorman@nvamos.com.

Mobile information and consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared, sold, rented, transferred, or disclosed to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers, including telecommunications carriers and messaging infrastructure providers, may process limited information solely as necessary to transmit messages, prevent fraud, provide technical support, or comply with law. They are not authorized by Doorman to use SMS opt-in data for their own marketing.

How consent is obtained

There is no public SMS signup. The recipient opts in by deliberately configuring or authorizing their mobile number in the private Doorman application and enabling entry notifications. Consent is specific to this messaging program and may be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP or contacting doorman@nvamos.com.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, diagnose problems, document consent and opt-out requests, and meet legal or platform requirements. Logs may be deleted or anonymized when they are no longer needed.

Data security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your number by contacting doorman@nvamos.com, subject to any records that must be retained for legal, security, or compliance reasons.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when material changes are made.
